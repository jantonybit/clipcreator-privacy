ClipCreator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

This policy describes how the ClipCreator application (developed by Jantonybit) handles information.

1. We do not collect personal data

ClipCreator does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline and has no telemetry, analytics, tracking, ads or user accounts.

2. Recorded data stored locally

All screen, audio and image recordings are created and saved only on your own computer, in the folder you choose. Nothing is sent to any server.

3. Application preferences

Your settings (hotkey, destination folder, theme, language, quality and fps) are stored locally in %APPDATA%\ClipCreator\config.json. This file stays on your device and can be deleted by you at any time. When uninstalling, you may remove this file manually.

4. Permissions

  • Screen and sound capture: used only to create your recordings, always started by you.
  • Microphone: used only when you select an audio input.
  • File access: used only to save files in the folder you choose.

5. Third-party components

The app bundles FFmpeg (LGPL/GPL license) locally to process video and audio files. No data is sent to the FFmpeg project or any third party.

6. Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: jantonybit@gmail.com.
